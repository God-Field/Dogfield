# Non-original Flash interface images

These resources are local replacements made by `tools/flash/create-mode-images.py`,
not recovered official image files. Paths below are relative to
`public/flash/assets/images/`. This inventory includes the generator's legacy
compatibility outputs, even when those changes are being maintained separately.

The flat panels, help boxes, ticket marker, close buttons, and room-filter
checkboxes are locally drawn designs. Mode, lottery, and legacy controls reuse
official login/logout skins with locally chosen lettering and layout; that does
not make the resulting images official artwork.

The English Artifact Book controls reuse the official Japanese skins. Their
English lettering, font treatment, and restored background pixels are local
adaptations. “Artifact Book” and “Bead” come from the embedded English text;
category translations and draw-button wording were supplied locally. The entrance
button shifts its text down two pixels when pressed; controls inside the book do
not shift. Original Japanese files are preserved.

## Official artwork reused at other paths

The card-image aliases and HTML5-to-PNG conversions documented in
[FLASH-ASSETS.md](FLASH-ASSETS.md) reuse official drawings. They are substitutions
for missing historical files, rather than newly invented drawings. Shared Artifact
Book backgrounds, page grids, close buttons, and detail panels also remain
original artwork.
